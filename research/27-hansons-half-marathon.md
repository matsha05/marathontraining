## Overview
- **Author:** Kevin & Keith Hanson (Hansons-Brooks Distance Project)
- **Book:** "Hansons Half-Marathon Method" (2014)
- **Philosophy:** Cumulative fatigue, 6 days/week, 12-14mi long run cap

### Key Protocol Notes (Beginner Half)
- **Long Run Cap:** 12 miles (alternates 12/10 after Week 12)
- **Speed Phase (Weeks 6-10):** Tuesday intervals at 5K-10K pace
- **Strength Phase (Weeks 11-17):** Tuesday intervals at 10K pace
- **HMP Tempo Progression:** 3mi → 5mi at goal half marathon pace
- **Peak Mileage:** 42 miles (Weeks 12, 14, 16)

### Key Protocol Notes (Advanced Half)
- **Long Run Cap:** 14 miles (alternates 14/10 after Week 12)
- **Speed Phase (Weeks 2-10):** Starts Week 2 (vs Week 6 for Beginner)
- **Strength Transition:** Week 11 (10K pace intervals begin)
- **HMP Tempo Progression:** 3mi → 7mi at goal half marathon pace
- **Peak Mileage:** 50 miles (Weeks 14, 16)

## Key Differences: Half vs Marathon Hansons

| Aspect | Half Marathon | Marathon |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| Long Run Cap | 12mi (Beg) / 14mi (Adv) | 16mi |
| Tempo Pace | HMP (Half Marathon Pace) | MP (Marathon Pace) |
| Strength Pace | 10K pace | MP-10s |
| Peak Mileage | 42mi (Beg) / 50mi (Adv) | 57.5mi (Beg) / 61.5mi (Adv) |
| Run Days | 6 | 6 |
| Duration | 18 weeks | 18 weeks |

## Training Key Definitions

- **Rest/CT:** Rest or Cross-Train (elliptical, cycling, swimming, yoga, strength)
- **HMP:** Half Marathon Pace (your goal race pace)
- **HMP-10s:** Half Marathon Pace minus 10 seconds/mile (NOT used in half plans; this is for marathon)
- **10K Pace:** Your current 10K race pace (used for strength phase intervals)
- **5K-10K Pace:** Interval pace between your 5K and 10K race paces
