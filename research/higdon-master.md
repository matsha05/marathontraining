## Workout Definitions (Hal Higdon's Voice)

### Easy Runs
> "Run at a comfortable pace. You should be able to hold a conversation. If you can't talk, you're running too fast."

### Long Runs
> "Run 30-90 seconds per mile slower than your marathon pace. The purpose is time on your feet, not speed. Walking breaks are fine and encouraged."

### Pace Runs (Race Pace)
> "Run at the pace you plan to run in your goal marathon. This teaches your body to be efficient at race speed."

### Tempo Runs
> "Start easy for 10-15 minutes, then build gradually to near 10K effort. Hold that effort for the prescribed time, then ease back. The 'peak' is only held for 6-8 minutes maximum."

### Hill Repeats
> "Find a hill about 1/4 mile long. Run up hard, jog/walk down for recovery. Use a 3:1 ratio of up to recovery time at first."

### Intervals (800m)
> "Run on a track at faster than marathon pace. 400m recovery jog between repeats. If you want to run a 3:10 marathon, do your 800s in 3:10."

### Cross Training
> "Any aerobic exercise other than running: swimming, cycling, walking. Avoid sports with sudden or sideways movements. Keep it easy - 30-60 minutes."

### Rest
> "Take a day off from training. Rest is when your muscles rebuild."

### 3/1 Long Run (Advanced)
> "Run the first 3/4 of your long run easy, then pick up the pace for the final 1/4. Not race pace - just 'steady.' Do this pattern at most once every 3 weeks."

### Stepback Weeks
> "Every third week is a recovery week where long run distance drops significantly. This allows your body to absorb training before the next push."
