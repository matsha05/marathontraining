## Overview
- **Author:** Kevin & Keith Hanson (Hansons-Brooks Distance Project)
- **Book:** "Hansons Marathon Method" (2012, revised 2016)
- **Philosophy:** Cumulative fatigue, no 20-miler, 6 days/week

### Key Protocol Notes
- **Speed Phase (Weeks 6-10):** Tuesday intervals at 5K-10K pace
- **Strength Phase (Weeks 11-17):** Tuesday intervals at MP minus 10 seconds
- **Long Run Pattern:** Alternates 16/10 after Week 10, max 16 miles
- **Tempo Progression:** 5mi → 10mi at goal marathon pace

### Key Protocol Notes
- **Speed Phase (Weeks 2-10):** Starts Week 2 (vs Week 6 for Beginner)

- **Long Run Pattern:** Alternates 16/10. Maximum: 16 miles
