## Base Training (10-12 Weeks)

Base training plans are designed to build a foundation BEFORE starting a race-specific program. They bridge the gap between off-season/casual running and structured marathon preparation.

---

### Base Training Novice

**Overview:**
- **Duration:** 12 weeks
- **Run Days:** 4 (Tue, Wed, Thu, Sun)
- **Walk Days:** 1 (Sat - 30 min)
- **Rest Days:** 2 (Mon, Fri)
- **Peak Long Run:** 8 miles (Week 10)
- **No quality workouts** — pure aerobic base

**Weekly Schedule:**

| Week | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|------|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 1 | Rest | 3 mi | 3 mi | 3 mi | Rest | 30 min walk | 3 mi |
| 2 | Rest | 3 mi | 3 mi | 3 mi | Rest | 30 min walk | 4 mi |
| 3 | Rest | 3 mi | 3 mi | 3 mi | Rest | 30 min walk | 3 mi |
| 4 | Rest | 3 mi | 3 mi | 3 mi | Rest | 30 min walk | 5 mi |
| 5 | Rest | 3 mi | 3 mi | 3 mi | Rest | 30 min walk | 3 mi |
| 6 | Rest | 3 mi | 3 mi | 3 mi | Rest | 30 min walk | 6 mi |
| 7 | Rest | 3 mi | 3 mi | 3 mi | Rest | 30 min walk | 3 mi |
| 8 | Rest | 3 mi | 3 mi | 3 mi | Rest | 30 min walk | 7 mi |
| 9 | Rest | 3 mi | 3 mi | 3 mi | Rest | 30 min walk | 3 mi |
| 10 | Rest | 3 mi | 3 mi | 3 mi | Rest | 30 min walk | 8 mi |
| 11 | Rest | 3 mi | 3 mi | 3 mi | Rest | 30 min walk | 4 mi |
| 12 | Rest | 3 mi | 3 mi | 3 mi | Rest | 30 min walk | 5 mi |

**Key Patterns:**
- Midweek runs constant at 3 mi
- Long run alternates: short (3 mi) / build (increases 1 mi)
- 2-week build / 1-week stepback pattern
- Final 2 weeks are recovery before starting race program

- **Strength Days:** 2 (Tue, Fri - combined with running)

### Base Training Advanced

**Overview:**
- **Duration:** 12 weeks
- **Run Days:** 6-7 (daily)
- **Strength Days:** 2 (Mon, Fri)
- **Stretching Days:** 1 (Wed)
- **Rest Days:** Optional (Mon/Sat on race weeks)
- **Peak Long Run:** 10 miles (Weeks 6, 8, 10)
- **Quality Sessions:** Hills, Fartlek, Tempo, Intervals (200m/400m)
- **Tune-up Races:** 5K (Weeks 5, 11), 8K (Week 7), 10K (Weeks 9, 12)

**Weekly Schedule:**

| 1 | 3mi+str | 5×hill | 3mi+stretch | 40m tempo | 3mi+str | 30m fartlek | 6 mi |

| 5 | 3mi+str | 9×hill | 3mi+stretch | 30m tempo | 3mi+str | 3mi/Rest | **5K Race** |
| 6 | 3mi+str | 10×hill | 3mi+stretch | 45m fartlek | 3mi+str | 30m fartlek | 10 mi |
| 7 | 3mi+str | 16×200 | 3mi+stretch | 30m tempo | 3mi+str | 3mi/Rest | **8K Race** |
| 8 | 3mi+str | 10×400 | 3mi+stretch | 45m fartlek | 3mi+str | 30m tempo | 10 mi |
| 9 | 3mi+str | 16×200 | 3mi+stretch | 30m tempo | 3mi+str | 3mi/Rest | **10K Race** |
| 10 | 3mi+str | 10×400 | 3mi+stretch | 45m fartlek | 3mi+str | 30m fartlek | 10 mi |

| 12 | 3mi+str | 10×400 | 3 mi | 30m fartlek | 3mi/Rest | Rest | **10K Race** |

**Key Patterns:**
- Tuesday alternates: Hills (Weeks 1-6) → Track intervals (Weeks 7-12)
- 200m weeks: 7, 9, 11 | 400m weeks: 8, 10, 12
- Thu alternates: Tempo (odd weeks) / Fartlek (even weeks)
- Monthly tune-up races provide structure
- Hill reps build 5→10 over first 6 weeks
- Weeks 11-12 drop volume, add rest before final races

### Spring Training

**Overview:**
- **Duration:** 10 weeks
- **Run Days:** 4 (Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat)
- **Cross-Train Days:** 2 (Wed bike, Sun cross)
- **Strength Days:** 2 (Tue + Sat)
- **Rest Days:** 1 (Mon)
- **Peak Long Run:** 90 min (Week 9)
- **Quality Sessions:** Intervals (400m), Tempo, Time Trials
- **Time Trials:** Monthly (1500m → 3K → 5K → 8K → 10K)

**Weekly Schedule:**

| Week | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|------|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 1 | Rest | 30m run+str | 30m bike | 6×400 int | Strength | 50 min LR | Cross |
| 2 | Rest | 45m run | Cross | 30m run+str | 30m tempo | Strength | **TT 1500m** |
| 3 | Rest | 30m run+str | 30m bike | 7×400 int | Strength | 60 min LR | Cross |
| 4 | Rest | 45m run | Cross | 30m run+str | 40m tempo | Strength | **TT 3000m** |
| 5 | Rest | 30m run+str | 40m bike | 8×400 int | Strength | 70 min LR | Cross |
| 6 | Rest | 45m run | Cross | 30m run+str | 40m tempo | Strength | **TT 5000m** |
| 7 | Rest | 30m run+str | 40m bike | 9×400 int | Strength | 80 min LR | Cross |
| 8 | Rest | 45m run | Cross | 30m run+str | 50m tempo | Strength | **TT 8000m** |
| 9 | Rest | 30m run+str | 40m bike | 10×400 int | Strength | 90 min LR | Cross |
| 10 | Rest | 45m run | Cross | 30m run+str | 50m tempo | Strength | **TT 10000m** |

**Key Patterns:**
- 2-week rotation: Odd weeks = intervals/LR, Even weeks = tempo/TT
- Time Trial distances increase monthly (1500m → 10K)
- Long runs in MINUTES (50-90 min progression)
- 400m intervals build 6→10 reps
- Tempo builds 30→50 min
- Heavy strength focus (2×/week)
- Designed for off-season fitness maintenance

## 5K Plans (8 Weeks)

### 5K Novice

**Overview:**
- **Duration:** 8 weeks
- **Run Days:** 3 (Tue, Thu, Sat)
- **Walk Days:** 1 (Sun)
- **Optional Days:** 2 (Mon, Wed - Rest or run/walk)
- **Rest Days:** 1 (Fri - Absolute rest)
- **Peak Long Run:** 3 miles (Week 7)

**Weekly Schedule:**

| Week | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|------|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 1 | Rest/r-w | 1.5 mi | Rest/r-w | 1.5 mi | Rest | 1.5 mi | 30 min walk |
| 2 | Rest/r-w | 1.75 mi | Rest/r-w | 1.5 mi | Rest | 1.75 mi | 35 min walk |
| 3 | Rest/r-w | 2 mi | Rest/r-w | 1.5 mi | Rest | 2 mi | 40 min walk |
| 4 | Rest/r-w | 2.25 mi | Rest/r-w | 1.5 mi | Rest | 2.25 mi | 45 min walk |
| 5 | Rest/r-w | 2.5 mi | Rest/r-w | 2 mi | Rest | 2.5 mi | 50 min walk |
| 6 | Rest/r-w | 2.75 mi | Rest/r-w | 2 mi | Rest | 2.75 mi | 55 min walk |
| 7 | Rest/r-w | 3 mi | Rest/r-w | 2 mi | Rest | 3 mi | 60 min walk |
| 8 | Rest/r-w | 3 mi | Rest/r-w | 2 mi | Rest | Rest | **5K Race** |

**Key Patterns:**
- Saturday distance = Tuesday distance (alternating progression)
- Sunday walk builds +5 min/week
- No quality workouts (pure aerobic base)
- Taper: Week 8 Saturday rest

**Key Patterns:**
- 3-week rotation: Easy → Tempo → Pace
- Tempo builds +10 min/cycle (30, 40, 50, 60 min)
- Long run repeats distances for 2 weeks before building
- Cross-train builds 30-60 min parallel to long run
- "3-day" plan actually requires 5 active days
- Taper: Week 12 minimal running, Sat rest

## Implementation Notes

1. **Long run units vary**: Some plans use miles, Advanced plans often use minutes
2. **3/1 pattern**: Already implemented in our `higdon.ts` — confirmed match with HM Advanced
3. **Pace runs**: Goal race pace for the target distance
4. **Tempo definition**: Easy start (5-10 min) → build to near 10K pace → easy finish
5. **Tune-up races**: Replace long runs; provide Sat rest before Sunday race
6. **Cross-train**: Non-impact aerobic (cycling, swimming, walking)
7. **Base Training purpose**: Build foundation before starting race-specific programs
8. **Spring Training**: Maintains fitness during off-season; heavy strength focus
